## 1. Aims / Goals of the Site

1. **Generate Leads** — Drive contact form submissions from both private and commercial clients in the Main-Kinzig / Wetterau region.
2. **Build Trust Quickly** — Show real work, real people, real testimonials. No stock photos. Visitors should feel "these people are reliable" within seconds.
3. **Showcase Full Service Range** — Make it obvious that AK Crystal Clean is a one-stop shop ("Alles aus einer Hand") so customers don't look elsewhere.
4. **Highlight Differentiators** — Quality checks by owners, 98% client retention, fast emergency deployments, family culture, eco-friendly products.
5. **Simplify Contact Flow** — Short form (name, email, phone, service checkboxes) + automatic 24h confirmation so Mehmet can handle inquiries on his schedule.
6. **Strengthen Brand Recognition** — Reinforce the crystal/clean/sparkle identity with the existing purple logo while modernising the overall look (less purple, more contrast).
